Accessibility

Last updated: 5 November 2026

What the game supports: sound and vibration can be turned off; screen reader labels on menus; a Reduce motion option in Settings; tap areas of at least 48 on menus.

Known limitations: Infect City is a real-time action game played by steering with the thumb, so gameplay itself requires vision and touch and cannot be played with a screen reader. In-game buttons may be smaller than 48. The menu labels have not yet been tested with a real screen reader.

Contact: if something blocks you from playing, write to r.b.a.app977@gmail.com.
